Methodology

The UKPI combines 12 indicators across five pillars, each weighted by how directly it reflects economic stability and public confidence.

PillarWeight
Macro & Fiscal — CPI, GDP growth, public debt20%
Monetary & Markets — Bank Rate, FTSE volatility, GBP FX volatility20%
Labour — unemployment, Job Security Proxy15%
Confidence & Sentiment — CCI, Business Barometer, EPU (blended)25%
Social & Wellbeing — crime, purchasing power, wellbeing20%

Normalization. Every indicator is z-scored against its own trailing 10-year mean and standard deviation. Series where a higher raw value is beneficial are direction-corrected so a higher final score always means more panic.

Mixed-frequency alignment. Monthly/daily indicators align to a shared reference month. GDP growth, crime rate, and wellbeing update quarterly at best and always report their own latest available figure instead.

Volatility indicators. FTSE and GBP FX volatility are derived from daily price data as the standard deviation of daily returns, accumulated monthly over time.
